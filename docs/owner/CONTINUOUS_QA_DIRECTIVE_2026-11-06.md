## 1. Mission and standing

- Roles: lead architect, QA engineer, mathematical verification, security, performance, integration coordinator.
- Functionality alone is insufficient: independently verify that every feature does what the owner intended,
  produces correct results, integrates with the platform, is reliable under realistic conditions, and does not
  deteriorate as features are added.
- **Permanent and automated**, part of every future development cycle, **cross-model** (no Claude-only testing
  rules). Not a one-time exercise. Goal: from a rapidly built AI-assisted application to a mature, continuously
  verified platform.
- Authorized: investigate, implement, test, repair and improve engineering and verification infrastructure within
  existing governance and owner-authorized scope. Do not stop at a report or proposal — implement the highest-priority
  authorized improvements through the normal test, review, integration and deployment workflow.
- Enhances the 2026-11-06 sitewide remediation campaign (its verification requirements and gaps); must not create a
  competing roadmap, a duplicate issue inventory, or interfere with production recovery and time-sensitive evidence.

## 3. Execution, reporting and limits

- **Start immediately**: assess repository state, review #2080, reconcile the standard into the agent and roadmap
  system through the merge process, then begin the feature-intent verification campaign inside the remediation work;
  an accurate initial census and the highest-risk test-coverage gaps; do not wait for a perfect inventory; select the
  first practical batch, reproduce, implement real corrections with retained tests. Prefer working, reviewed,
  verifiable improvements over more planning documents. Keep observed evidence, implementation, integration,
  deployment and production verification distinct.
- **Routine technical choices are delegated** (consistent with MD-38): choose by correctness, predictive quality,
  security, maintainability, performance, reliability and evidence. Escalate only owner-reserved decisions,
  unavailable permissions, unsafe actions or other documented gates.
- **Progress report at meaningful checkpoints**, eight fields: (1) features and critical paths inventoried;
  (2) requirements verified against actual behavior; (3) confirmed defects and test-quality gaps; (4) tests added or
  strengthened; (5) repairs with exact commits and PRs; (6) actual test results and independent review outcomes;
  (7) changes merged and deployed, with production evidence; (8) remaining unverified behavior, blockers, next
  authorized work. No unsupported claims of comprehensive verification.
- **Final objective**: every feature has a defined purpose and independently verifiable behavior; regressions are
  detected automatically; mathematical correctness is demonstrated; predictive effectiveness is measured; performance
  is monitored; unnecessary code is safely eliminated — and the discipline is embedded so every future Claude, Codex
  or other session follows it. "Do not just audit the platform. Build the system that keeps the platform correct."
- Limits carried from the governing records and restated by this directive: no protection bypass or failing-candidate
  merge; no secrets, private data or unauthorized access; no provider-restriction bypass; no weakened controls or
  tests; no unbounded production load; no consequential methodology change without its gates; respect active work
  claims and the remediation campaign's priority order.
