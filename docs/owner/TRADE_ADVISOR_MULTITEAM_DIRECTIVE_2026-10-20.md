## OWNER AMENDMENT — PUBLIC TRADE ADVISOR FAIRNESS-ONLY RESULTS (supersedes the public ACCEPT/DECLINE requirement)
The public Brisket Trade Advisor must NOT tell users to ACCEPT, DECLINE, APPROVE, DENY, or make/pass on a trade. It answers one neutral question: **Is this trade fair?**
Public results:
- **FAIR TRADE** — the trade falls within our predetermined fairness threshold.
- **NOT FAIR** — the trade falls outside our predetermined fairness threshold.
- **UNABLE TO DETERMINE** — the necessary valuation or ownership evidence is insufficient.
Applies to 2-, 3- and 4-team trades. For multi-team transactions, evaluate each participating team's fairness separately; the overall trade is FAIR only when all participating teams meet the established fairness criteria; otherwise NOT FAIR, provided sufficient evidence exists.
Use existing canonical player valuations and the active geom_q078_v1 package-adjustment methodology; reuse the authoritative fairness-band owner — no competing formula. Investigate and validate the appropriate predetermined threshold, including its behaviour for small trades, deep packages, picks, IDP and multi-team transactions; do not silently inherit unsuitable provisional thresholds or arbitrarily choose new ones; bring any required methodology change to the owner for approval. The threshold is fixed, versioned, deterministic and server-owned.
The public Advisor is a market-fairness tool, not a personalized advice tool. The authenticated private Trade Calculator and War Room keep comprehensive YES/NO recommendations (roster context, competitive strategy, replacement levels, forced drops, all relevant factors). Both share the same canonical valuations and package mathematics; their final outputs are intentionally different.
No numerical player values, adjusted gaps, private formulas or other proprietary information publicly.
Update #1861, #1866, the API contracts, frontend language, multi-team implementation, tests, design documentation and launch criteria accordingly. All other authorized implementation, privacy, validation, multi-team support and release instructions remain in effect. Proceed; keep the public launch behind explicit owner approval.
