## Experience ambition

Build a coherent, unmistakably custom, world-class, expert-facing football personnel and market intelligence experience: NFL GM/scouting room × elite fantasy analyst × quantitative research lab × financial trading workstation × high-end sports broadcast. It should feel plausible to show at the MIT Sloan Sports Analytics Conference; capable of impressing expert sports analysts through useful information architecture, chart quality, rigor and editorial/visual craft, not decorative complexity. Inspirations for *qualities to study, not copy*: ESPN/SportsCenter urgency and narrative, The Wall Street Journal editorial credibility, PFF/advanced NFL analytics detail, Bloomberg/financial-terminal density, thoughtful scientific visualization, and football executive/scout workflows. NFL analysts such as Robert Mays exemplify substance and analytical communication, not a request to impersonate or reproduce anyone's work.

The interface should feel like a professional application / operating environment, not a generic AI-generated SaaS dashboard or a patched-together collection of prototypes. Distinctive but restrained typography; excellent number rendering; authentic information density; meaningful heatmaps, tables, distributions, trend graphics, comparisons, relevant player/context visuals; fast access to freshest useful data; responsive mobile and desktop interactions; minimal visual noise; disciplined hierarchy and clarity.

## Research questions (no preconceived answers)

- Why do AI-generated sites converge? Separate reproducible observations from hypotheses about token priors, training examples, prompts, framework defaults, component kits, ubiquitous fonts, generic dashboard patterns, speed/evaluation incentives, and iterative consistency failures. Develop tested prompt and production controls that avoid template convergence without making the UI gimmicky.
- Evaluate a **darker or dark-first** future direction, while comparing evidence-based alternatives including charcoal, slate, ink, black and warmer neutral systems. Consider long-session visual fatigue, text/data/chart contrast, accessible semantics, color-vision deficiency, glare, typographic readability, legibility, performance and mobile contexts. Dark interest is a strong preference, not a hex-code mandate.
- Compare current leading AI frontend builders and workflows (e.g. Gemini, Claude, ChatGPT/Codex, specialist design-to-code systems, human-designer-supported and mixed workflows) on identical real tasks. Assess originality, instruction compliance, maintainable code, design quality, responsive behavior, accessibility, visualization, CI fit, cost, licensing, and concurrent-team suitability. Do not select based on company reputation, presumed creativity, or demo screenshots.
- Evaluate the current Next.js/React frontend and Nginx deployment architecture based on evidence, performance, incremental migration, operational cost and backend/API separation. Nginx is a proxy/web server, not the page design system. No migration unless a measurable outcome justifies it.
- Design the site's future information architecture **from the bottom up**. Menus, route groupings, search, shortcuts, navigation, hierarchy, landing experiences, layouts and component systems are open to reconsideration. Evaluate existing routes, shipped and planned roadmap features, rather than working from a handful of screenshots.
- Make content actionable before explanatory. Public users should *use* rankings, trade analysis, scouting and news without first reading how the model was made. Prefer concise labels, plain-English output, layered disclosure and '?' help → searchable glossary/contextual help. Maintain honest data freshness, uncertainty, restrictions, units and attribution; retain deeper proprietary technical introspection only in authorized views.
- Distinguish public league sports-network/museum/game-day experiences from authenticated front-office, war-room and research tools, while sharing one brand, type/color/chart language, predictable interaction patterns, accessible navigation and reusable primitives. Different audience needs need not imply identical page geometry.
- News must become a useful editorial and live-information surface, not an undifferentiated RSS list: assess responsibly sourced headlines, breaking-news indicators, NFL reporter/newswire references, ticker(s), player/team impacts, relevance, timing, deduplication, provenance, rights/attribution and availability. Never fabricate or claim live/breaking without evidence.
- Account for planned future features and growth. Establish a common architecture that accommodates features still in the roadmap and does not require a bespoke design for every new page.

## Privacy, intellectual property and trust

Public UX should not expose proprietary algorithms, weights, source-combination recipes, confidential architecture, internal debugging, vendor credentials, private league/user data or competitive decision intelligence. Concealment requires **real server-side auth and data-minimization**, not merely hiding DOM elements. Necessary copyright attribution, licenses, terms-of-service compliance, source transparency where consequential to trust, data timestamps, uncertainty, limitations and non-misleading claims remain mandatory. Avoid implying that a proprietary method is independently validated if it is not. Public league routes and authenticated/private routes must be audited for access boundaries.

## Parallel development and governance

Backend and modeling work under Claude and the current approved remediation/QA program continue in parallel. Frontend discovery and prototype experiments should use isolated branches/worktrees and artifact boundaries, stable typed/versioned API contracts, fixtures, mock-data seams, shared integration checkpoints and clear work claims. A second AI may own the frontend **when chosen**; no two agents own the same files or invent competing models. Contract changes need a review handshake; feature-specific backend changes must not require frontend project suspension. Reconcile the existing always-parallel UI lane and route inventory, not a second source-of-truth backlog. Preserve working endpoints, tests, feature flags, product semantics and rollback paths. Cross-model rules in AI_INSTRUCTIONS, AGENTS, GEMINI and CLAUDE are shared and model-neutral.

The separate research effort should produce: cited audit of AI visual convergence; route-by-route frontend truth inventory (including public/auth/mobile); three or more distinct viable visual concepts with type/color/chart hierarchy samples; accessibility and quantitative readability checks; architectural options with costs/risks; a blind scored same-task AI workflow bake-off; recommended AI + toolchain with rationale and backups; IA sitemap/navigation blueprint; unified design tokens/components/data visualization/interaction specification; news UX design; phased, reversible frontend/backend-concurrent migration and verification matrix; guardrails preventing generic AI drift; and a **ready-to-send implementation prompt addressed to the recommended builder**.

Do not begin the broad UI rebuild solely because this document exists. The owner will review the research and pick the final direction/tooling before implementation is authorized in the execution plan. Current backend remediation, security and other owner gates remain intact.
