I approve the Phase 3 recommendation: KEEP GEOM_78, q = 0.78, unchanged.

I also authorize a separate, high-priority research program investigating the low-value-asset problem identified in
Phase 3.

The objective is to determine whether low-value assets are incorrectly valued on our canonical scale, whether their
usefulness as trade currency needs a separate adjustment, or whether the market's discount should remain informational
only.

Please investigate all three possibilities rather than assuming that a 2,000-point haircut is the correct solution.

Requirements:

1. Preserve current production values, weights, Value Adjustment, fairness classifications, and recommendations.
2. Preserve Phase 3's research evidence and frozen conclusions.
3. Preregister competing hypotheses, candidate treatments, validation methods, and success criteria before
   confirmatory evaluation.
4. Investigate the actual contribution of low-value players to lineup utility, replacement value, roster capacity,
   trade outcomes, and market value.
5. Distinguish usable roster depth from genuinely expendable filler. Avoid penalizing every player below an arbitrary
   threshold.
6. Test whether existing Team Context, forced-drop, and replacement-level logic already accounts for these effects. Do
   not double-count them.
7. Test players, IDP, rookie picks, and mixed packages separately. Report inadequate sample sizes honestly.
8. Run isolated shadow comparisons against GEOM_78 across the Trade Calculator, Trade Finder, Trade History, War Room,
   and public fairness-only calculator.
9. Specifically investigate filler assets flipping near-even verdicts and whether any candidate eliminates
   inappropriate flips without undervaluing genuine starters.
10. Protect the public fairness-only implementation from unapproved methodology changes. Its threshold research can
    proceed independently, but do not silently adjust public or private results.
11. Use independent chronological and league-disjoint evidence where possible, and distinguish historical market
    agreement from genuinely better trade advice.
12. Keep the new research in isolated worktrees and continue the public multi-team calculator, outlier V2, and all
    other authorized engineering work concurrently.

The goal isn't to make our calculator imitate trades people have historically accepted. The goal is to determine how
much low-value assets should actually contribute to a trade while preserving the importance of legitimate depth.

Deliver an owner report comparing the three approaches, with actual trade examples, mathematical properties, decision
changes, uncertainties, and an explicit recommendation.

Research and shadow experiments are authorized. Production changes are not.
