Mechanism: the contract's generic current-year rows carry rankDerivedValue=null
  "2026 Mid 1st"  -> rankDerivedValue None   (ktc 4581)
  "2027 Mid 1st"  -> rankDerivedValue 5606
  "2028 Mid 1st"  -> rankDerivedValue 4564
and the contract ships contract.pickAliases = {"2026 Mid 1st": "2026 Pick 1.06", ...}
precisely to redirect them onto the priced SLOT rows. league-analysis.js consumes
pickAliases; portfolio-insights.js does not, so it stops at the null row and
scores every current-year pick at 0.
