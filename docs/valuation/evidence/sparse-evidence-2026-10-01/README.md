## The method (candidate C)

A row the haircut would hit keeps its one family's observation as central
evidence. A family that did **not** list the player can bound it from above, at
that family's published cutoff for the position, but only when **all** of these
hold:

- the source is active, scope-eligible and dynasty-verified;
- it is not a rookie-only board for a veteran, nor a rookie-excluding board for a
  rookie;
- its board is healthy, has known full coverage and is on schedule;
- it ranked that position;
- the source did not publish the player's name;
- the player's identity is unique and not quarantined.

How the bounds combine:

- Absences count once per family, at the loosest member bound.
- Bounds below the observation enter the pipeline's own blend at their cutoff.
  That gives the upper end of what the evidence allows. It is not an invented
  last place and not a shrink toward zero.
- Bounds above the observation say nothing and are dropped.

Uncertainty stays with `src/api/confidence.py`: all 51 rows are `low`.

Candidate B (an independent prior) is not built, because none exists: KTC Market,
BDVM and yesterday's board all descend from the same evidence or are forbidden as
votes. Candidate D (withhold when no family can bound the row) withholds nothing
here, because every row had at least one witness family.
